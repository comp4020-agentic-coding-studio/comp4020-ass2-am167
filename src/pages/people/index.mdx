The studio is run by a convenor and three tutors, each of whom owns a stretch of
the semester, plus a visiting critic from practice who sits on the jury at every
assessed pin-up and nowhere else.

Take briefs, marks, extensions and adjustments to the convenor. Take a build
that will not behave to the tutor whose weeks you are in. The full routing is on
the [policies page](/policies/#where-to-ask-what).
