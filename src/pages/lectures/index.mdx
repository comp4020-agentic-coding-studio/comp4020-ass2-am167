Twelve Monday lectures at 10:00, from 22 February to 24 May, with a two-week break after Week 6. Each prepares the afternoon studio: read that week’s brief and bring its named save or worksheet. Jury weeks require completed work before the noon upload.

The [resources and access guide](../resources/) explains teaching-room notices, lab access, worksheets and the evidence labels used in every deck. On phones, decks read as a continuous document; on desktop they retain the presentation view.
