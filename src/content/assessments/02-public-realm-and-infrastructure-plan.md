The district after the break, argued as parks, plazas and streets planned with
the utility networks beneath them, and funded from the closure you set in Week
8. Livability is the heavy criterion. Fiscal performance is where an unfunded
plaza actually loses marks.

This brief is the sole authority for submission, display and defence. The Week
9 studio is the jury. It does not add a second memo length, a second view list,
or a live-save contract of its own.

## What you submit, Monday 3 May 12:00

Upload, as one artefact:

- **8–12 in-simulation screenshots**, captioned with in-game day and time.
  Include at least one attractiveness overlay, one land value overlay, one water
  or sewage info-view, and one street-level view at **19:00** in the evidence
  pack's fine-weather state, taken from the issued camera against a marked
  polygon. Count people inside the polygon during one in-game minute and write
  the count on the caption. A count with no time of day and no weather state is
  not evidence.
- **A defence memo of no more than 1,200 words**, structured against livability,
  fiscal performance and mobility performance in that order. Confront the public
  realm spend with whatever remains unfunded on the treatment plant.
- **One costed monthly line for every public space proposed**, funded from the
  Week 8 closure. Associated happiness or land-value movement is labelled
  associated, not isolated cause.
- **The signed unfunded list** from Week 8, as the memo's last sheet.
- **The save** `<student ID>-w09`, matching the wall.

Late work follows the [studio policies](../../policies/) artefact clock.

## What you hang, 13:00–13:25

Printed A3 is the default. An accessible or on-time digital equivalent occupies
the same roster bay and uses the same clock. Memo pinned, not on a laptop. The
unfunded list last. Hang in **roster order**. The hang closes at 13:25.

## Defence

**14:00**, Studio 2.14. Tobias Wren chairs; Sunniva Marek sits as Visiting
Critic. The Course Convenor is not in the room this week; the week's tutor of
record chairs. **Four minutes** per plan at the wall: ninety seconds for one
claim, the rest for questions. Changeover is inside the four minutes. This
clock is the brief's override of the ordinary crit protocol.

The question the wall has to survive: did the district get better because you
designed something, or because you spent money, and what did each associated
point of happiness cost per month.

## Marking

Livability 45, fiscal performance 30, mobility performance 25. An unfunded
plaza is a fiscal fail, not a livability one. Keep the save exactly as
submitted; Week 10 reads this district, not a weekend repair.
