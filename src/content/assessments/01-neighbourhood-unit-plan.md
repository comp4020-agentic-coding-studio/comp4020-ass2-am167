One residential cell on Halstead Reach, at Perry's dimensions, designed to a
walkable amenity standard, argued as a neighbourhood unit. The cell is the
ground you zoned in Weeks 1–3 and upzoned around a named amenity core before
this deadline. The jury has not watched you build it.

This brief is the sole authority for what you submit and how you are defended.
The Week 4 studio is the jury. It does not invent a second list of views, a
second word count, or a production exercise after 12:00.

## What you submit, Monday 15 March 12:00

Upload, as one artefact:

- **A defence memo of no more than 800 words.** Name the two metrics you traded
  and the element of the plan where that trade is visible. Cite the Week 1 site
  report for why the unit sits on the Reach. Cite the Week 2 block dimension and
  its upkeep. Cite the Week 3 scheme and the named front door.
- **In-simulation screenshots**, captioned with in-game day and **08:00**,
  including at least: the land value overlay over the cell; the coverage radius
  of **every** service building you sited, each captured with that building
  selected; the traffic volume layer showing the Kerrow corridor on the cell
  boundary.
- **The save** `<student ID>-w04`, matching the screenshots.

Work uploaded after 12:00 is late under the [studio policies](../../policies/)
artefact clock.

## What you hang, 13:00

An **A3 plan** of the cell with the district-tool boundary visible, the amenity
core at Pelling Cross, and the Kerrow corridor drawn where it actually runs.
The three required views printed beneath it. A card at the bottom of the bay
stating, as two figures in ₡, what the cell costs per month and what it returns
per month — allocated on the issued district-cost worksheet, not described as a
native per-district budget.

Hang in **roster order**. Work that is not on the wall at 13:00 is marked from
the 12:00 upload alone.

## Defence

**14:00**, Studio 2.14, one bay run in two rounds of eight. Marisol Quaye
chairs both rounds; Sunniva Marek sits as Visiting Critic throughout.
**Six minutes** per plan: two presenting, four questions. This clock is the
brief's override of the ordinary five-minute crit protocol; the evidence does
not change.

The first question is always: which two of the three metrics did you trade, and
where on the wall is that visible.

## Production is not the pin-up

Upzoning the Pelling Cross frontage, reading each service coverage from the
selected building, and comparing an inside-cell clinic with a shared-corridor
clinic on the issued three-cell scaffold, are **homework completed before
12:00**. Do not overwrite `<student ID>-w03` to make those branches; keep them
as disposable comparison files. The session after 13:00 is hanging and jury.
Nothing built after 12:00 changes the artefact.

Perry's exclusion question — who the school catchment leaves out — is asked in
the Week 4 **lecture**, before this deadline, so it can still change the plan.

## Marking

A plan that closed a shortfall without recording which move closed it marks
in the middle band and no higher.
