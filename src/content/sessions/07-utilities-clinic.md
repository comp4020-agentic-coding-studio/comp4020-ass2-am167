## Before you come

Bring the unmodified `<student ID>-w06` and its off-machine copy. Also bring the
issued `kerrow-ref-w07-overload` checkpoint, the failure chronology with the
first entry you would investigate marked, and the evidence pack’s water, sewage
and electricity availability figures.

Re-read Week 6’s metrics and the Week 3 paragraph that sent industry to
Ombersley Flats as downwind, downstream and cheap. Have the water and sewage
views open at 13:00. The session starts from recorded capacity, not a symptom
remembered from an unattended run.

## What has already happened

In the supplied reference scenario, city water consumption exceeds production
and sewage production exceeds treatment capacity. The Farrant Row treatment
plant is overloaded, and the pollution overlay shows exposure in the Kerrow
below its outfall.

Keep the causal record split. Siting the Pellow cells at Ombersley was a student
design decision. The stress-test arrangement is an instructor-supplied
condition. Production and treatment falling below demand are observed capacity
shortfalls. The panels do not support claims about pipe pressure, per-pipe
capacity, a far-end-first outage, backflow or a boil notice. Connectivity and
flow direction do not become hydraulic pressure because the story would be
more dramatic that way.

## In the room

The opening round asks when you would investigate and which live field would
trigger you. Nadia Ilkhom writes **noticed** and **available capacity** as two
columns on the board. It is legitimate to mark “not yet”; it is not legitimate
to invent an in-game day you did not observe.

The overload checkpoint then goes on the projector with water and sewage views
open. The room distinguishes water availability, sewage-treatment load and
receiving-water pollution before entering four groups of four. Each group gets
ten minutes with Nadia. One student opens the panels, one reads the chronology,
one challenges the causal claim and one records the correction; roles rotate in
the comparison. All sixteen students therefore have a named diagnostic task.

## What you compare

Use the same overload baseline for two dated, disposable branches. The Wensley
Bank water-production increment is allocated at **₡6,800/month** and restores
water headroom; it does not increase sewage treatment. The Farrant Row treatment
increment is **₡5,100/month** and restores treatment headroom; it does not add
water production.

Record both panels on both checkpoints. Return to the personal save and place
neither facility. Choose the order in which they should be funded and justify
it on the reverse-salient card. The reference water-first branch’s +₡900/month,
happiness 61% and Ombersley land value 11 are reference-scenario values, not
outcomes your personal save is required to reproduce.
