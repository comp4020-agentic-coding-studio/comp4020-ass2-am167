## The clock

memo is due Friday 21 May at 12:00 for both chairs to pre-read. The artefact is
due Monday at 12:00. Hang from 12:15, draw the running order at 12:50, begin the
jury at **13:00** and remain for the joint close through 16:00.

Each defence is **fifteen minutes**, not twenty, and the two rooms start at
13:00, not 14:00. A missed jury is marked from the memo and boards without a
live defence.

## Your fifteen minutes

The protocol is five minutes uninterrupted, clarifying questions, critique,
then the presenter speaks last. The five-minute walk-through has five moves:

1. Full district, roads only: name the site and the constraint that set the
   plan — 40 seconds.
2. Full district, land value: say what the plan did and where it landed — 60
   seconds.
3. Corridor at neighbourhood scale: state the claim and its grounds — 120
   seconds.
4. One block at street level: say what it is like to stand there — 60 seconds.
5. Full district, metric readouts: name the trade — 60 seconds.

Do not cut the street-level move. It is the only minute not delivered by a
panel. A metric card is permitted; spatial evidence on the boards is required.

## The overlays, in order

Open land value first: who captured the uplift? Open public transport routes
and ridership second: what happens at the terminus? Open service coverage by
district third: who sits outside the radii? Open budget fourth: in which year
does the surplus end? Open water, ground pollution and fire hazard last: what
happens at Ombersley Flats?

All five questions get asked. The named overlays, corridor budget column,
unfunded list and Week 10 exposure statement on the boards must support what the
walk-through says.

## What the jury asks

The jury asks which of **fiscal performance**, **mobility performance** and
**livability** you traded, and where that choice is spatially visible. It will
set the district mean land value of 40 against a core at 66 and Ombersley at 18.
It will ask whether the ₡186,000 reserve is held for year-fifteen stability or
spent on the forecast Kerrow cross-town link, and which mobility gap the choice
accepts.

The last question has two halves. Does the district hold under Week 10’s fire
load case? Answer with `d3` against `recovered`, not with an account of a flood.
What could the model not show? Answer from the Week 11 appendix, curve and
Farrant Row trace. Transit share and walk share keep separate observation
denominators and are never added. The reserve/link result is a worksheet
forecast, not a live UI field.
