Tobias modelled municipal budgets for six years. They now mostly think about
benches. It is a shorter journey than it sounds: a bench is a line item with a
frontage. They teach
Weeks 8 and 9: the fiscal week, where a service cutback is imposed on your
district and you decide what it costs, and the public realm week, where you find
out how little of a convincing street is actually expensive.

They are the person to see when your district is technically solvent and nobody
would want to live in it, which is a more common outcome here than students
expect. Livability and fiscal performance are two of the three metrics, and
Weeks 8 and 9 are where the studio stops letting you pretend they point the same
way.

Ask about the plaza that cost more than the transit line. You will get both sets
of numbers.
