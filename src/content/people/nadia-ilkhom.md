Nadia is a water engineer by training and still thinks like one: everything in
the city is a network with a capacity, and every capacity has a curve that gets
ugly at the end. Most of their practice career went on trunk sewer upgrades in
districts that had been zoned for growth nobody had costed the pipes for.

Weeks 7 and 10 are theirs: the infrastructure lecture and failure-cascade
walkthrough that turn the studio from what a district looks like into what it
can carry, and the hazard scenario after the break that finds out whether any
of it held.

Their clinic is worth booking even when nothing is broken. Half the utility
failures in this studio are visible in the capacity readouts three weeks before
they become visible in the streets.
