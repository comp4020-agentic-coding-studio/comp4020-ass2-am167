# Adversarial review — studio session plans

**Reviewed:** 2026-09-02  
**Scope:** all twelve plans in `notes/studio-plans.md`, checked against the
matching lectures, assessments, policies, people and course through-line. This
is a review-only handoff; no studio plan or session body was revised.

Three fresh reviewers independently attacked Weeks 1–4, 5–8 and 9–12. The
findings below collate their feedback by implementation priority. Line numbers
refer to the reviewed snapshot; search for the quoted phrase if they drift.

## Verdict

**Not approved for production.** The Kerrow Basin through-line, named places
and recurring fiscal/mobility/livability tradeoffs make this a distinctive
course rather than a generic syllabus with game nouns substituted into it.
Several individual crit moves are excellent.

The plans are not yet safe for a future agent to turn into pages. Their evidence
pack is missing, the weekly bodies do not consistently obey their own evidence
rules, several exercises cannot fit the available studio or simulation time,
the personal-save/reference-save chain is contradictory, and assessed sessions
invent requirements before the assessment briefs exist. Weeks 2, 4, 7–8, 10
and 12 require structural revision rather than copy-editing.

## Release blockers

### 1. Build the empirical substrate before writing session pages

The plans correctly require a pinned game build, Kerrow map, reference
checkpoints, worksheets, source labels and timing pilots
(`notes/studio-plans.md:142-158`). None exists in the repo, while every week
quotes exact shared outcomes.

**Future-agent action:** create and pilot the promised evidence pack. Until a
number is reproduced, label it a worked reference value rather than a result
students will obtain.

### 2. Propagate the evidence labels through every weekly body

The opening convention distinguishes live UI, manual observation, worksheet
calculation and reference-scenario value (`:124-140`). Later tables revert to
unsupported labels such as native mobility/travel-time/mode-share readouts and
per-district budget fields (`:771-775`, `:1158-1164`, `:1540-1547`, with the
same pattern in Weeks 9–12).

**Future-agent action:** audit every “Where it is read” cell. Name the inputs
and formula for every derived measure. Keep passive coverage, simulated service
reach and walking catchments separate.

### 3. Write the assessment briefs before their studio sessions

All three assessment bodies are placeholders, but Weeks 4, 9 and 12 already
specify word counts, print formats, view sets, evidence and jury rules. Week 9
even notes that it may out-specify the A2 brief (`:3833-3851`).

**Future-agent action:** make each brief the sole authority for submission and
defence requirements, then reconcile the lecture and studio plans to it.

### 4. Define one week-by-week save and evidence chain

The course promises one continuous personal district, but Weeks 5–8 often read
issued checkpoints while personal saves retain different interventions. Week 6
says no unattended run (`:2488-2500`) yet its page plan and hand-off still
require a six-day `w07-pre` save (`:2545-2549`, `:2573-2580`). Week 7 replaces
that run with supplied evidence; Week 8 then assumes utility work may have been
built.

**Future-agent action:** add a matrix with, for each week: personal save in,
reference checkpoint, personal change committed, reference-only observation,
reading source and personal save out.

### 5. Resolve operations once, not separately in each week

The plans disagree with policies or themselves about jury start times,
presentation clocks, room allocation, staffing, pre-reading and accessibility.
The policies promise fifteen machines for a cohort of sixteen, while several
weeks require sixteen simultaneous live saves.

**Future-agent action:** centralise the jury and lab rules, secure or account
for the sixteenth workstation, name all booked rooms and staff, and give each
after-class upload an actual destination and late/access rule.

### 6. Pilot the schedule in the actual lab

Week 4 asks for two long simulation branches outside an already full jury run
sheet; Week 10 asks for a nineteen-day recovery run inside one studio. Several
other weeks rely on save loading, settlement or data extraction with no
measured allowance.

**Future-agent action:** record wall-clock run and load times for every
checkpoint-based exercise. Replace long waits with staged saves or pre-recorded
traces.

## Cross-course risks

- **False choice.** The Reach, fine grid, Scheme A, straight bus and widening
  are presented as decisions even when the course requires one answer. Name a
  canonical lab operation honestly; reserve “choice” for branches the course
  can carry and assess.
- **Authorship disappears in the middle.** Weeks 5–8 increasingly prescribe the
  same move or leave the student's save unchanged. Preserve at least one
  consequential student-authored change in each non-jury week.
- **Missing software prerequisite.** The site promises no prior software
  experience, but students are expected to build, branch, measure, export and
  recover saves almost immediately. Add induction and a recovery exercise in
  Week 1.
- **Formulaic crit rhythm.** The repeated one-sentence metric confession and
  “asked again” ritual risks becoming punitive and makes different studios feel
  identical. Keep the metric spine but vary the operation: prediction,
  counterfactual, audit, diagnosis, budget defence, causal limitation and
  omission.

## Week-by-week findings

### Week 1 — Site Selection and the First Grid

- The two sites are called defensible, but the rest of the course requires
  Halstead Reach. Either support both canons or call the Ridge a costed rejected
  alternative.
- Pumping/head costs and scenic land-value benefits need an explicit worksheet
  or must be described as planning judgement, not simulator evidence
  (`:343-358`, `:394-415`).
- Add software induction, save recovery and an accessible Week 1 alternative
  before independent building is required.
- **Preserve:** reading five layers before drawing and naming what the chosen
  site bought and deferred.

### Week 2 — Block Pattern and the Walkability Desk Crit

- **Blocked:** Week 1 hands forward an unzoned network, but Week 2 expects
  traffic, queues, redistribution and walk share (`:491-499`, `:681-759`). Use
  populated paired checkpoints or turn this into a graph/path-count exercise.
- Fix one controlled block comparison; the current arbitrary dimensions and
  80/160/240 m variants do not isolate network form.
- Do not ask students to delete, rebuild, settle equal populations and observe
  another game day inside the current clock. Stage the states.
- **Preserve:** the topology question and paired comparison.

### Week 3 — Zoning the First District

- **Blocked:** the intake is placed downstream of the sewage outflow
  (`:1120-1134`) and later treated as a delayed Week 7 failure. Put the normal
  intake upstream and create any failure in a separate labelled checkpoint.
- Scheme A is declared the assessed branch before the comparison
  (`:1015`, `:1038-1054`). Call Scheme B a counterfactual or support either
  branch downstream.
- Separate ground, air, water and noise evidence; do not rely on one identical
  “plume” story or unpiloted exact outcomes.
- **Preserve:** the named front-door route and “which layer rules this out?”
  style of defence.

### Week 4 — Upzoning the Amenity Core

- **Blocked:** the run sheet is entirely jury, yet the later exercise asks for
  upzoning, coverage tests, two clinic branches, long runs and readings
  (`:1424-1435`, `:1479-1547`). Move that production before the deadline or
  redesign this as jury only.
- Reconcile the six-minute defence with the standing critique policy and book
  the second jury bay, wall length, displays and acoustic separation.
- Keep passive service coverage, a walking-time catchment and declared
  one-third clinic-cost allocation distinct.
- Move Perry's exclusion/equity critique before A1 is due.
- **Preserve:** separate core and edge readings rather than hiding them in a
  district mean.

### Week 5 — Designing the Transit Spine

- The straight six-stop bus is a prescribed common intervention, not the result
  of an open charrette. State that clearly and preserve one authored access or
  stop decision.
- Keep headway and cost explicitly manual/worksheet-derived and tie the fixed
  ridership and queue values to the evidence pack.
- Treat service thresholds and waiting-time multipliers as planning heuristics
  to test, not simulator laws.
- **Preserve:** fleet/headway arithmetic and the distinction between 600 m
  zoning and 400 m network walking distance.

### Week 6 — Peak-Load Stress Test

- **Blocked hand-off:** delete the stale six-day unattended `w07-pre` path; it
  contradicts the run sheet's checkpoint method and accessibility rule.
- The proposed hourly decomposition cannot be produced and defended in its
  small group slot. Issue it as data to audit or simplify the output.
- Label the exact widening trajectory as a reference scenario, not a universal
  induced-demand result.
- Make the instrument selected at the close matter later.
- **Preserve:** immediate versus settled comparison and instrument auditing.

### Week 7 — Utilities Clinic

- **Blocked:** the body now avoids the earlier hydraulic/boil-notice fiction,
  but the metric spine and related hand-offs still retain pump upgrade, boil
  notice, backflow and six-day contamination claims (`:202`, `:2668-2688`,
  `:2835-2838`). Reconcile the studio, lecture and spine around one tested
  utility scenario.
- Decide whether water-first, treatment-first or neither is committed to the
  personal save; Week 8 cannot work until this is explicit.
- Complete the budget bridge from +₡1,800 to +₡900 when a ₡6,800 monthly cost is
  added, or stop attributing that balance to the upgrade alone.
- Remove the universal 95%-then-cliff claim unless the evidence pack proves it
  separately for each system.
- **Preserve:** diagnostic tree, reverse-salient card and the distinction
  between observed evidence and instructor-supplied diagnosis.

### Week 8 — Budget Under Cutback

- **Blocked by Week 7:** line zero protects/reverses utility increments whose
  status is undefined (`:3041-3044`, `:3303-3307`).
- Correct the bus arithmetic: a 24-minute round trip cannot become roughly a
  ten-minute headway by removing whole vehicles from a four-bus fleet; the
  stated saving also needs a shown formula.
- A “nearest” reference branch cannot test an arbitrary student closure. Limit
  choices to exact branches or make mismatch analysis the explicit exercise.
- Teach and specify everything A2 needs before its Week 9 noon deadline.
- **Preserve:** “the amount is imposed; the composition is chosen,” the sealed
  prediction and the signed unfunded list.

### Week 9 — The Public Realm Build

- **Blocked:** write A2 first; the session currently invents its required memo,
  display, 19:00 observation, unfunded list and live-save contract.
- Week 9 requires a Week 8 walk share that Week 8's reading table does not
  establish (`:3435-3437`).
- Reconcile the money: +₡3,400 minus ₡2,200 and ₡340 is +₡860, not +₡1,200.
  The missing ₡340 bridge must be shown or the values corrected.
- Ockley Green is at 60% maintenance, not “stopped maintaining.”
- Keep plaza/tree results as associated values unless controlled branches
  isolate them.
- Add movement/changeover and real pre-reading time to the exact four-minute
  jury arithmetic.
- **Preserve:** confronting the public-realm spend with the still-unfunded
  treatment plant and the causal warning on cost per associated point.

### Week 10 — Hazard Scenario

- **Blocked:** replace the flood premise with a supported, tested hazard, or
  explicitly distribute a custom scenario and disclose the altered setup. The
  present water-source control is an editor operation, not the ordinary-game
  setting the plan claims (`:3930-3938`).
- The day 0–19 run cannot fit the studio. Use staged checkpoints or a recorded
  recovery trace.
- Days 0–19 are twenty rows, not nineteen; the text also briefly changes the
  cohort from sixteen to twenty.
- Reconcile Week 10's residential exposure with Week 3's industrial Pellow
  cells and choose one studio/lecture hand-off.
- Treat exact failure order, duration and recovery as reference evidence, not
  guaranteed mechanics.
- **Preserve:** distinguishing an event reading from normal district state and
  requiring measured failure evidence rather than the most dramatic image.

### Week 11 — Reading the Land-Value Curve

- “Eleven weekly saves, `w01` to `w10`” is ten saves
  (`:4237-4245`, `:4471`, `:4498`). Students arrive with ten and create the
  eleventh here.
- Opening ten saves and extracting two series in 35 minutes needs pre-extracted
  evidence or prework with a recovery path.
- Replace “the move that produced it” with temporal association/hypothesis; the
  session's own model-literacy lesson rejects unsupported single-cause claims.
- Guarantee the earlier Farrant Row household evidence or reconstruct it from a
  known checkpoint.
- Reconcile the lecture and studio curve scales and their Week 10 gap.
- **Preserve:** “all three improved; now find the metric you do not have,” and
  the omissions appendix.

### Week 12 — Final Jury

- **Blocked:** boards are hung in presentation order before that order is drawn
  (`:4624-4625`). Draw first or hang in fixed roster order.
- Two chairs cannot meaningfully read all sixteen noon submissions by 12:50
  while supervising setup. Give jury copies an earlier pre-read deadline.
- Swapping chairs for the last two slots does not make every student “seen by
  both” (`:4647-4651`). Add a credible cross-room moderation/calibration
  protocol or reconfigure the jury.
- Name and book the actual panel: only Quaye and Marek are listed, while the
  prose promises semester tutors in both rooms.
- Write the capstone brief before specifying its memo, appendix, boards,
  screenshots and defence.
- Reconcile 13:00/14:00 starts, 15/20-minute clocks and accessibility across the
  brief, policy, lecture and studio. Week 4 did not use a 20-minute slot.
- Define mutually exclusive mode-share denominators before adding transit and
  walking; document the long-range budget/link forecast.
- Permit a metric card and require spatial planning evidence so memory and oral
  polish do not dominate a 50% holistic assessment.
- **Preserve:** the fourth question about what the model cannot show and the
  ambition to defend the semester as one position.
